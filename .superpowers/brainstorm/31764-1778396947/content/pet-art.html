Bump the template canvas from 8×11 to roughly 9×22. Pet has real silhouette: each species gets a recognizable body shape (fuzzy oval, ghost cloud, mech rectangle, blob droplet, crystal facet, glitch fragments). Eyes/mouth/pattern slots stay; they just have more room to breathe. Highest fidelity, most "this is a creature" feel.

3 — Block sprites (~10×16)

Built from half-block characters (▀▄▌▐█). Looks like an 8-bit sprite translated to ASCII. Solid, readable silhouettes. More "rendered" than line-art. Each species gets a distinct block-shape body. Risk: blocks render font-dependently in some terminals.

Each direction is just one species (fuzz) sketched. Whichever you pick, the actual work would extend it across all six species — and the per-species silhouette differences would be more pronounced (mech blocky, ghost wispy, crystal angular, etc.) than they appear in the placeholders.
