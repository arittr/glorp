A — Calm command-line

No frame, no chrome. Just the program's output, like running tree or btop's minimal mode. Sections separated by ─ vitals ─ rules. Pet floats in space. Lots of breathing room. Quiet. Reads like a CLI tool that respects your terminal.

C — Two-pane dashboard

Thin rounded corners on two side-by-side cards. No outer chrome. Pet on the left, data on the right. Reads like a focused dashboard. Densest of the three. The 7-day sparkline gets a real spot. Header is one tight line.

Things deliberately consistent across all three: the warm palette (amber accent, moss green good, coral bad), ░/█ bar chars, lowercase labels, terminal-clock timestamps. Things deliberately different: amount of frame, density, vertical vs horizontal split.
