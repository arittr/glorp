# Glitch Metamorph Art — Design

**Date:** 2026-07-02
**Status:** direction approved by Drew in visual brainstorm ("way, way better"); hero
lineup and sequencing recorded below as defaults pending his spec review.

## Goal

Give the glitch species the same treatment blob and crystal received: a distinct
shape-class per evolution stage instead of one scaled rectangle, blob-level detail
density, whole-silhouette asymmetry so the facing flip reads, and a living face at
every stage including the elders. Drew's driving feedback, verbatim:

- "i don't want it to just be a block, blob has so much more detail and character
  than the current glitch pets"
- "still really basic compared to the blob and theres not enough asym"
- "B has the most inter and intra species variation"

Direction chosen: **B, "digital metamorph"** — each stage is a different digital
object, with the stage names (bit → byte → packet → thread → glitch → daemon →
kernel) driving the shape classes.

## The through-line

The packet-box is the creature's **childhood shell**, not its body. S2 pops the
lid; S3 tows the empty frame like a wagon; S4 wears its displaced slabs; S5 keeps
one panel as a glowing chest-dock; S6 half-sheds it like a hermit-crab carapace.
Species identity rides on lens eyes `◉`, acid-green palette, static shading, crumb
bits, and the box *history* — no longer on being a box.

## The seven templates

Candidates were generated by a 10-agent design fleet (two competing briefs per
contested stage, 22 total), each machine-validated for 11×8 display grid and stage
cell band, and curated in a visual review with Drew. Cells = occupied cells at the
canonical slot fill; asym = cells that change under mirroring (blob S6 = 34, the
prior benchmark). Rows below are the spec — transcribe verbatim into
`src/pet/art.rs` (`{eyes}` = 3 display cols, `{mouth}` = 1).

## Design rules honored

- 11 display cols × 8 rows exactly; stage cell bands per `STAGE_CELL_BANDS`.
- Closed, flood-fillable silhouettes; dense `▒▓█` figure-ground (no `░`-only body).
- Living face: `{eyes}` + `{mouth}` slots at S2–S6 (S5/S6 regain the mouth the
  current elders lost — this delivers "re-slot elder expressions", approach 1 of
  the persistent-corruption spec's elder-charm requirement).
- Whole-silhouette asymmetry at S3+ (scores 44/60/57/76 vs blob's 34).
- Every row structurally distinct from its neighbors; ≥3 outline glyph families;
  directional shading, light from upper-left; a focal feature beyond the face.
- Existing daemon-lens mood faces, blink profile, particles, palette: unchanged.

## Renderer changes

1. **`mirror_char` extension** (`src/tui/panels/pet/art_lines.rs`): add the block
   pairs `▛↔▜`, `▙↔▟`, `▌↔▐`, `▖↔▗`, `▘↔▝`, `▚↔▞`. Today only `()/\<>[]{}bd`
   swap, so block-built asymmetry mirrors with wrong glyph shapes. Benefits every
   species; flows to watch, companion, and menubar through the shared render seam.
   Mirrored-frame goldens/snapshots will need regeneration — review diffs, don't
   bulk-accept.
2. **Interior texture pinning per (species, stage)**: glitch S3/S4 keep the seeded
   `▒↔▓` swap (static bodies; per-individual noise is on-brand and finally has
   canvas), glitch S5/S6 are pinned like crystal (hand-shaded gradients scramble
   under swaps — the `d80d22d` lesson). Mechanism: extend the pin check in
   `apply_interior_texture` from per-species to per-(species, stage).
3. **New glyph audit**: the templates introduce `╪` and `═` (S3/S6) plus `▪ · ◆ :`
   already used elsewhere. Verify width-1 rendering on the target terminals and
   in the companion's AppKit path during preview-lab review.

## Sequencing with `private/glitch-persistent-corruption` (default: branch first)

Land the persistence branch as-is, then this art pass on top:

- `safe_glitch_patch_candidates` / `is_protected_glitch_face_cell` re-derive
  against the new templates. The span-based parts adapt automatically; the
  hardcoded S5/S6 protected expression island is **deleted** — real `{eyes}`/
  `{mouth}` spans now exist at every stage, so the generic Eye/Mouth-span
  protection covers the elders.
- Repair marks need no new logic: the new bodies offer natural weld sites (the
  `╪`/`═` seams, panel joints, carapace edge); persistence fixtures re-validate
  in the preview lab.
- Corruption visibility note: on textured bodies the `GLITCH_NOISE` glyph swap
  has less glyph contrast than on the old hollow frame; visibility rides the
  acid-green `Corruption` role recolor. Confirm it pops in preview fixtures.

## Testing and rollout

- Update `glitch_base_art_passes_phase1_invariants` expectations (new cell ramp)
  and the `glitch_resting_face_is_alive` S4 assertion text ("dense closed packet"
  no longer describes S4 — assert density + closed silhouette on the new form).
- Full `cargo test` (not `--lib` only — dev-preview snapshots and structural caps
  live in integration tests), `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets
  --all-features -D warnings`.
- Preview lab is the go/no-go gate: regenerate `pet` scenario fixtures, visual
  review of species-stage matrix, live-states, and glitch persistence fixtures
  before merge. Companion check requires rebuilding the app bundle
  (`node scripts/build-macos-companion-app.mjs`), not just cargo.
- One-time visual change for live glitch pets, same precedent as the overhaul;
  no identity/state migration.

## Out of scope

species' art. The full 22-candidate archive (including unused alternates) lives in
`.superpowers/brainstorm/70621-1783016793/glitch-art-candidates-all22.json`
(gitignored); the two closest-call alternates are recorded below for posterity.
