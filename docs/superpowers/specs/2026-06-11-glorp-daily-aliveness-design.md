- Status: direction approved by Drew; spec pending review
- Builds on:

  - `2026-06-09-glorp-lives-in-time-design.md` (DayContext: phase, tiredness,

## Problem

Glorp already has a real day/alive system. The Alive Room work derived a clean
`RoomLifeProfile` and a `PetPerformance` state, animates scene moments, and
varies the **sky** layer by phase. Yet the scene still reads as nearly the same
across the day. The reason is specific: the Alive Room implementation delivered
the derivation, the motion punctuation, and the sky/cue layers, but **stopped
short of the resting-state differentiation the spec called for**. Most of the
day no tokens are flowing, so the resting state is what a user actually sees —
and it varies only through tiny one-cell cues and subtle breath changes.

The signals already exist and are reliable. The gap is in the render consumers:
they do not yet *use* what is derived.

| | Already shipped | Spec intended, not yet built |
|---|---|---|
| Room / day | Phase drives the **sky** glyph family + density (`sky_palette_for_phase`, `phase_count_scale`, `pet.rs:268/303`); seasonal hue drift; 7-day climate tint | Phase driving the **room interior** composition and a scene-wide **warmth/contrast** tint. `room_glyphs_for` (`room.rs:425`) takes no `day_phase`. |
| Pet | One-cell performance **cue** glyph (`pet.rs:1046-1059`); tiredness-driven slower breath and fewer blinks | The spec's *"slight lower posture, softer eyes, curled resting posture"* — no posture offset or eye substitution exists. |

Goal: the resting scene looks meaningfully different through the day and across
kinds-of-day, with every difference traceable to a real signal — finishing the
Alive Room intent, plus two correctness/micro-life gaps it never covered.

## Principles

- **Locked boundary (inherited, reaffirmed).** Wall-clock/calendar time may vary
  visual *texture* but never *personality*. Which behaviors, postures, eyes, or
  speech the pet shows must key off a real attribute (tiredness, today_ratio,
  `PetPerformance`), never the clock alone. Phase-driven palette warmth is
  texture and is allowed; phase-driven *behavior* is not.
- **Rhythm-led, clock-tinted.** `DayPhase` comes from the rhythm-derived
  `PhaseWindows`, or clock-defaults when the ledger is immature (`day.rs:77`).
  Only the palette *warmth* gets a light wall-clock nudge so "dawn" reads as
  morning light even on an unusual day.
- **No new derived contract.** The signals live on `RoomLifeProfile` and
  `DayContext` already. This work extends the render consumers; it does **not**
  add a parallel `DayAmbiance`/profile layer.
- **Real data only.** No autogenerated flavor. Texture may be date-seeded, but a
  real signal always selects *which* texture shows.
- **YAGNI.** No new pet art templates. Posture and mood use offsets, overlays,
  and eye substitution over existing 11×8 templates (Alive Room spec, Pet
  Performance section).

### Three composition rules (the crux)

1. **Phase tints and composes; biome owns.** `room_glyphs_for` gains a
   `day_phase` input. Biome glyphs remain the room's identity; phase shifts
   scene warmth/contrast, scales interior glyph density, and biases composition
   (e.g. dawn light from one side). Layered, never overriding — mirrors how the
   sky layer already consumes phase.
2. **Day-scale is the slow baseline; activity is the fast lift.** The pet's
   resting brightness and posture baseline come from `PetPerformance`
   (tiredness/today_ratio). The existing per-poll `activity_level` lift and the
   `energy<0.6` vital droop compose **on top**, in this order: performance
   baseline sets resting brightness/posture → `activity_level` lifts from there
   → vital droop clamps only when genuinely starving. A tired evening pet still
   visibly brightens when work arrives; it just settles back lower than a
   fresh-morning pet.
3. **Bold room, subtle pet.** Room warmth/contrast/density swing wide
   (e.g. interior density ~0.5→1.0 across phases). Pet posture moves at most one
   resting row, eye substitution fires only for clearly-distinct states, and the
   brightness baseline stays in a narrow band. The world tells time; the pet
   stays a pet.

## Non-goals

- No new species art templates or morphs.
- No countdowns, ETAs, "peak hours," or streak counters (tamagotchi spirit).
- No change to the data pipeline, ledger, calibration, or `DayContext`
  derivation — this is render-layer work plus one new mood and one cadence
  table.
- No re-architecture of `RoomLifeProfile` or `PetPerformance`.

## Architecture

All four directions are changes to **render consumers** of already-derived
state. No new module, no new poll-time derivation.

Data flow (unchanged upstream): `build_watch_view_model` builds `DayContext`
and the view model → `derive_room_life_profile` (`room.rs:144`) produces
`RoomLifeProfile` (carries `pet_performance`, weather, biome) → render consumers
read both.

Touched consumers:

- `room.rs::room_glyphs_for` and its `biome_glyphs`/`weather_glyphs`
  (`room.rs:425/635/712`) — gain `day_phase` for interior composition + tint.
- `panels/pet.rs` pet render path (`apply_pet_performance_cues`,
  `pet.rs:1006`; the lightness/lift seam) — gains posture offset + eye
  substitution + the performance brightness baseline.
- `pet/animator.rs::species_breath_rhythm_decis` (`animator.rs:478`) and
  `BreathRhythm::Asleep` (`animator.rs:445`) — cadence re-align + sleep depth.
- `game/metabolism.rs::Mood` (`metabolism.rs:25`) and
  `pet/render.rs::expression_for` (`render.rs:215`) — new `Ecstatic` mood.

## A — Room phase composition and warmth

Extend the room interior to vary by phase, the way the sky already does.

- **Signature change.** `room_glyphs_for` and the biome/weather glyph builders
  take `day_phase: DayPhase` (and the rhythm/clock-tint inputs needed for
  warmth). Today they take only `RoomLifeProfile`, `area`, `now`,
  `color_capability`.
- **Interior density by phase.** Apply a phase scale to the interior motion
  budget (parallel to `phase_count_scale`, `pet.rs:303`): dawn sparser/waking,
  day full, dusk settling, night quiet. Bold swing (rule 3).
- **Warmth/contrast tint.** A scene-wide palette bias: dawn cool, day neutral-
  bright, dusk warm/lower-contrast, night dim. Composes with the existing
  `season_hue_drift` and `climate_tint` (`pet.rs:319/337`) rather than
  replacing them — phase is the light, season/climate are slower tints on top.
  The warmth bias is the one place a light wall-clock nudge applies.
- **Composition bias.** Where layout allows, dawn admits light/glyphs from one
  side (spec: *"light enters from one side"*); night reduces interior glyphs and
  favors cozy behavior. Biome identity glyphs always remain (rule 1).
- **Flat/low-color mode.** Differentiate by glyph family and placement, not RGB
  alone (Alive Room overlay requirement). Warmth tint is a no-op in flat mode;
  density/composition still differ.

## B — Pet performance posture and eyes

Extend `PetPerformance` rendering past the one-cell cue to the spec's intended
posture/eye vocabulary, using overlays and substitution — never new templates.

- **Brightness baseline (rule 2).** Each performance state sets a resting
  brightness/saturation baseline beneath the existing activity lift and vital
  droop. `RestedAwake` neutral; `TiredAwake`/`HeavyDayCozy` slightly lower and
  warmer; `AsleepDreaming` dim.
- **Posture offset.** A resting vertical offset of at most one row and a small
  reduction in wander range: tired/cozy/asleep settle lower and roam less;
  burst/perk lifts briefly. Reuses the frame-positioning and wander seams; no
  template edits.
- **Eye substitution.** Where a species' art supports it, substitute softer/
  closed/dream eyes for tired/cozy/asleep (spec: *"softer eyes where species art
  supports it"*). If a species cannot, it falls back to motion/breath only
  (spec rule). Closed/dream eyes for `AsleepDreaming` reuse `closed_blink_eyes`
  (`render.rs:177`) and the existing `hold_eyes_closed` path.
- **Keep the cue.** The one-cell cue glyph stays as a small reinforcing signal;
  posture/eyes carry the readable difference.

All of B keys off `PetPerformance`, which is derived from real attributes — no
clock dependency (locked boundary).

## C — Breath cadence re-align and Ecstatic mood

Two correctness gaps versus the pet.jsx source of truth. Not part of Alive Room.

### C1 — Breath cadence re-align

`species_breath_rhythm_decis` (`animator.rs:478`) diverges from `SPECIES_ANIM`
(`pet.jsx:605`): Rust breathes slower across the board, and **ghost and blob are
inverted** — fast-breathers in the source, slow-breathers in Rust. CLAUDE.md
requires porting from pet.jsx; the divergence has no recorded rationale, so we
re-align, preserving pet.jsx proportions at ~200 ms per fast-tick (decis =
`breathPeriod` × 2, inhale = `breathHold` × 2):

| species | pet.jsx (period/hold) | current decis | re-aligned decis |
|---|---|---|---|
| glitch | 9 / 2 | (20, 4) | (18, 4) |
| ghost | 11 / 3 | (45, 10) | (22, 6) |
| blob | 13 / 5 | (50, 12) | (26, 10) |
| fuzz | 16 / 4 | (40, 8) | (32, 8) |
| mech | 17 / 4 | (40, 5) | (34, 8) |
| crystal | 19 / 6 | (60, 8) | (38, 12) |

The ~200 ms/tick scale is the design choice; final feel is confirmed by eye in
the preview lab before commit. Breathing speeds up overall and the
glitch→crystal ordering matches the source.

### C2 — Ecstatic mood

The mood ladder tops out at `Happy → Content` (`metabolism.rs:239-241`), where
pet.jsx has `happy → ecstatic`. `Content` renders the pet's default trait eyes
(`render.rs:229`), so the source's distinct excited face is absent. Add
`Ecstatic` above `Happy`, triggered at peak vitals:

- Mood enum + string round-trip (`metabolism.rs:25/59`).
- Vitals threshold above the `Happy` tier in the mood derivation.
- Expression (`render.rs:215`): eyes `*o*`, mouth `▽` (`pet.jsx:322`).
- Blink-block (`pet.jsx:625` lists `ecstatic`): add `Ecstatic` to the
  blink-suppressed set alongside `Sad`/`Sleepy`/`Wilted`.

## D — Sleep-depth breath and idle laziness

Small micro-life on signals we already hold.

- **Sleep-depth breath.** `BreathRhythm::Asleep { onset }` (`animator.rs:445`)
  already carries time-since-onset. Deepen it over `onset`: the longer asleep,
  the shallower and slightly more irregular the breath — settling from light
  to deep sleep. Bounded; never stops.
- **Idle-gesture laziness.** Scale idle-gesture spacing by
  `PetLifeProfile.idle.idle_minutes`: gestures (wander excursions, twinkle)
  space out as idle grows, so a long-idle pet reads as settled rather than
  loop-frozen. Texture-only spacing change; no new behaviors introduced
  (locked boundary).

## Error handling and fallback

Pure presentation transforms of validated state; no new I/O or failure modes.
Before the ledger is mature, `DayPhase` uses clock-defaults (existing) and
`PetPerformance` resolves to `RestedAwake`, so a brand-new pet looks calm and
well. Flat/low-color terminals get density/composition/posture differences but
no RGB warmth. Unknown/odd inputs resolve to the neutral baseline, never a
broken frame.

## Testing and preview

- **Unit tests.** Phase → interior density/warmth mapping (A); performance →
  posture offset / eye substitution / brightness baseline (B); the
  composition-order test for rule 2 (performance baseline + activity lift
  compose without clamping surprises) — the subtle one; re-aligned breath value
  assertions and glitch→crystal ordering (C1); `Ecstatic` face + blink-block and
  the peak-vitals threshold (C2); sleep-depth breath monotonic over `onset` and
  idle-spacing growth (D).
- **Preview lab.** Add `dev-preview` scenarios crossing phase
  (dawn/day/dusk/night) with kind-of-day (fresh vs heavy) and a couple of
  performance states, at compact and normal watch sizes, including one small
  early-stage and one large late-stage pet (Alive Room preview requirement).
  These let us tune "bold room / subtle pet" by eye before shipping. Resting
  frames prove differentiation at rest; strips prove motion.
- **Flat-mode coverage.** A low-color preview/test proving phases differ without
  RGB.

## Sequencing

One spec, phased build (each phase independently shippable and preview-gated):

1. **A — room phase composition + warmth.** Headline; lowest risk; extends an
   established pattern.
2. **B — pet posture + eyes.** Builds on A's preview fixtures for tuning.
3. **C — breath re-align + Ecstatic.** Independent; can land any time.
4. **D — sleep-depth + idle laziness.** Polish.

## Open questions

None blocking. The ~200 ms/fast-tick breath scale (C1) and the exact warmth/
density swings (A) are tuned by eye in the preview lab during implementation.
